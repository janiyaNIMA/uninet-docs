### Society Committee / ExCom Dashboard
Purpose: Operational command center for society leaders and event coordinators.  
Key Components:
    Event post creator (upload flyers, set target module tags, assign required skills).  
    Active recruitment drive managers.  
    Quick-action buttons to boost promotions to targeted student feeds. 

### Automated Committee Recruitment Pipeline
Purpose: Streamlines finding and matching talent for organizing committees (OC) and executive committees (ExCom).  
Key Components:
    Showing all students who applied to the perticular event or the OC commity and AI-matched student candidate suggestions for special events and OC commities based on required skills, past contributions and academic background.  
    Candidate review cards showing student badge levels and engagement history.  
    Direct interview/recruitment invitation dispatch system that allows one click recruitment.

### Participation & Diversity Analytics Screen
Purpose: Higher-level data view for society leads and faculty admins.  
Key Components:
    Event registration and attendance analytics.  
    Demographic tracking metrics to specifically identify and encourage underrepresented groups (such as female undergraduates).  
    Overall campus participation trends.
