### My Profile Screen
Purpose: A centralized view for users to inspect and update their active profile data.
Key Components:
    Student header card (Name, University, Faculty, Degree Stream).  
    Editable skills, interests, and academic combination tags.  
    Quick status summary showing earned badge level (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) and total engagement points.  
    Direct link to generate/download the verified co-curricular portfolio.  

### Account Settings Screen
Purpose: A unified user management hub where students, society leaders, and administrators can customize personal information, profile assets, site themes, and platform preferences.
Key Components:
    Personal Information Editor: Editable fields allowing users to update their profile picture, contact email, and mobile phone number.
    Site Theme & Appearance Controls: Instant theme toggle supporting Light Mode, Dark Mode, and High Contrast System Default options.
    Notification Preferences: Customizable toggles to filter in-app alerts, email updates, and targeted society recruitment notifications.        
    Account Security: Password modification tools, active session manager, and secure account logout.
