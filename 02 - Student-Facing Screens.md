### Personalized Smart Feed (Home Dashboard)
Purpose: 
    The main hub replacing WhatsApp broadcasts and generic notice boards with targeted recommendations.

Key Components:
    AI Recommendation Cards:
        Ranked society updates, upcoming competition flyers, and workshops showing dynamic match percentage scores based on student profile data. 
        Filter and search options by module, society category, or interest tag. 
        One-click registration buttons for matched events. 

### Society Discovery Directory
Purpose:
    A centralized matrix showcasing all available campus organizations and student chapters. 
    
Key Components:
    Detailed society profiles highlighting purpose, past events, and executive committee members. 
    Skill & Benefit Mapping:
        Explicit callouts showing skills gained (e.g., leadership, full-stack dev) by joining.         
    WIE Mentorship & Inclusivity Spotlight:
        Dedicated section highlighting female student leaders, alumnae, and female faculty mentors for structured guidance.

### Event Detail & One-Click Registration View
Purpose:
    Dedicated page for register workshops, hackathons, or sports meets. 

Key Components:
    Detailed schedule, venue, speaker info, and prerequisites. 
    Auto-filled registration form pulling directly from the student's Smart Profile (eliminating "form fatigue").  
    Instant confirmation status and google calendar sync.

### Gamified Badge & Co-Curricular Portfolio Screen
    Purpose: 
        Tracks and showcases individual student growth and participation history over time.  

    Key Components:
        Digital Badge Display: 
            Visual progress and unlocked tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum).  
        Public community profile setting to display achievements.  
        Verifiable Co-Curricular Resume/Export: 
            A downloadable portfolio summarizing soft skills, event organization, and leadership roles for university job fairs and corporate recruiters
